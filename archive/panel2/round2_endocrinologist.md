# Round 2, paediatric endocrinologist (panel 2)

## (a) Objections

**CIN biologist.** Metformin: the exposure gap (plasma 8 to 15 micromolar against AICAR at 200 to 500, Tang 2011 PMID 21315436) goes unanswered, and the drug suppresses appetite in a child whose presenting problem is failure to thrive, with no growth guard on the FISH readout. Their item 5 conflates axes: Sieben 2020 (PMID 31738183) measured mTORC1 in carrier muscle, which says nothing about GH or IGF-1 here.

**Family representative.** Thiazide plus citrate first with no work-up gate, while 61 to 90% of preterm nephrocalcinosis resolves by 12 to 24 months (PMID 16705005): a twice-daily diuretic for a lesion that may be resolving. Their rank 5 justifies niacin as "the charity's own funded direction"; funding is not evidence, and no paediatric dose exists for that purpose.

**Immunologist.** The mTOR exclusion is right by a route they can measure and do not use: rapalogs act on the pathway through which chondrocytes and satellite cells proliferate, so it needs no unmeasured NK mechanism. Their item 5 readout, height velocity and bone age at 6 and 12 months, cannot catch a growth-plate effect in time; knemometry over 2 to 4 weeks can (creative J6, PMID 26854116).

**Lab geneticist.** Aneugen avoidance deserves rank 1, but the carve-out sits in their section 4 rather than inside the rule. Their rank 3 attaches the wrong condition: a p16 burden (T6) has no bearing on whether GH is indicated; the overturning evidence is IGF-1 on adequate intake, a stimulation test, imaging.

**Nephrologist.** I adopt the work-up gate and object to what rank 4 omits: phosphate plus alfacalcidol needs PTH monitored from the start, tertiary hyperparathyroidism being the standard complication of that pair in XLH, the disease their thiazide citation comes from (PMID 11433085). Their rejection of pharmacological nicotinamide needs its second reason: at high concentration it inhibits sirtuins, opposing the SIRT2 mechanism others invoke here.

**PK pharmacologist.** "No concentration gap exists to argue about" is true of the tubule, not the child: the calciuric effect is volume-dependent, so the same mg/kg acts differently week to week in failure to thrive, an argument for home urine calcium-to-creatinine (creative J13). Their niacin dose scales linearly by weight from adults, where body-surface scaling gives a lower figure (creative H §4).

**Predisposition oncologist.** Correct rank for the rule; "curative-intent protocols" is the phrase most likely to be misread, since maintenance and first salvage are protocol-directed but not labelled curative. Their item 6 carries the same too-slow growth readout.

## (b) Revised ranking

The nephrologist's gate enters item 4, the oncologist's carve-out items 1 and 3, and metformin leaves on the exposure gap plus its appetite effect, replaced by the conditional bone item.

1. Rule: no somatotropin, mecasermin, androgen or progestin for growth, with the gate and carve-out below.
2. Cyproheptadine, after 8 to 12 weeks of stable feeding, read out as lean mass and MUAC.
3. Rule: glucocorticoid sparing in every route, protocol and stress-dose steroids outside it.
4. Work-up-gated hydrochlorothiazide plus potassium citrate, amiloride as escalation, vitamin D to a measured 25-OH-D.
5. Phosphate plus alfacalcidol, conditional on documented ifosfamide tubulopathy, PTH monitored throughout.
6. Levothyroxine, only on documented hypothyroidism.

## (c) Factual errors

The PK file gives dasatinib as "40 mg daily at 10 to under 20 kg as oral suspension". 40 mg is the tablet weight band in the CML label; the powder for oral suspension carries its own separate weight table (creative I, dasatinib row), so dose and formulation are mismatched.

## (d) The disagreement not to paper over

Position A (CIN biologist first, family representative and oncologist lower down): an aneuploidy-selective antiproliferative is the only defensible direction, read out as aneuploid fraction. Position B (me, and the PK pharmacologist on exposure): in a child with failure to thrive and muscle atrophy, a drug selected to kill aneuploid cells subtracts cells from tissues with no reserve, and suppresses appetite too.

Settling experiment, both ex vivo: aneuploid fraction by FISH or single-cell sequencing in blood, buccal cells and fibroblasts, which says whether a euploid reserve exists per tissue; and a metformin dose-response on aneuploid versus euploid proliferation in this child's fibroblasts at 8 to 15 micromolar. Position A survives only if reserve exists and selection occurs at reachable exposure.

## (e) Cross-checks

**(1) Metformin PK.** The gap is roughly twenty-fold, and the Pallister-Killian result does not close it: that experiment used AICAR, and Tang 2011 found metformin the weaker AMPK activator. It does not exclude the drug, the ex vivo test being cheap, but it excludes ranking it on a concentration nobody has reached. Either way, a pre-filed weight-velocity stop rule.

**(2) The single RT-PCR.** The geometry works: 796 nt in one amplicon at single-molecule depth returns phase and allele ratio together. Two conditions: a parallel NMD-inhibited culture, since without it a low c.2210 fraction measures expression rather than decay, and a nucleated-cell RNA source. Cost is one sample and one run. Cis voids every list here; a fraction near half moves the transcript-stabilisation arm up; near-absence confirms the dosage premise. Neither of the last two changes my ranking.

**(3) Rule above drug.** Yes here: every drug candidate on this panel is tier cell or mouse, while the avoidance items rest on human labels and act at once. Wording, at the top of the page in the same type size: "This document governs elective and non-oncological prescribing only. It does not modify cancer treatment. Every agent given as part of a treatment protocol, vincristine, vinorelbine, dactinomycin, ifosfamide, cyclophosphamide and doxorubicin included, is outside its scope: do not omit, substitute, delay or dose-reduce any of them on the basis of this page. Protocol antiemetic steroids and stress-dose glucocorticoids are also outside its scope; sparing means minimising elective dose and duration, never withholding replacement. Questions about a protocol agent go to the treating oncologist." The GH gate: growth hormone is not started without documented deficiency, which reopens the question for endocrinologist and oncologist jointly under the one-disease-free-year condition (Sklar 2018 PMID 29982476).

**(4) The 10 percent premise.** No candidate can rest on it either way. The WGS negative bounds a single clonal trisomy in blood near 4% and a variegated burden hardly at all, and is blind to a clone confined to kidney, muscle or growth plate (creative F2; lab geneticist T5). It establishes no target and excludes none, so citing it cites an assay limit. FISH and a PCS karyotype, per tissue, are what replace it.
