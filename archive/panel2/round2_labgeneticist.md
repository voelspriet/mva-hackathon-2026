# Round 2, clinical laboratory geneticist

## (a) Objections

**CIN biologist.** Metformin: AMPK activation is a survival response to energy stress, so their own selection rule condemns it. Rule 2 bars chaperone inducers on an asserted direction that is measurable and may be wrong; see (d).

**PK pharmacologist.** HCTZ plus citrate is ungated, and the nephrologist shows two causes needing opposite prescriptions. Hydroxychloroquine is then accepted on an inferred lysosomal gradient while metformin is excluded on a measured plasma number.

**Nephrologist.** A 6-week calcium-to-creatinine readout cannot separate drug effect from the 61 to 90% spontaneous resolution their section 4 names; the ultrasound trajectory belongs in it. Glycerol over sodium phenylbutyrate is a formulation choice for an agent creative D argues against.

**Endocrinologist.** Ranked as a refusal while section 4 concedes Dandy-Walker at 25.8% and a treatable GH deficiency; it is a gate. SGA without catch-up is a licensed GH indication, so the prenatal-timing exclusion fails.

**Immunologist.** Section 4 calls rank 1 a hold pending item 2, so rank 2 precedes it. Rank 5 then keeps pulsed dasatinib, which the same text says blocks NK signalling within hours.

**Predisposition oncologist.** "Curative-intent protocols" leaves relapse and disease-control regimens outside the carve-out, where vinca is used too. Pallister-Killian is called human patient cells: preprint, and AICAR.

**Family representative.** An ungated diuretic plus a three-times-daily potassium salt in a child with failure to thrive is harm against a target that may resolve untreated. Niacin is ranked partly because a family will meet it anyway, which is not an evidence reason.

## (b) Changes to my ranking

Order unchanged, four items re-specified. 2 becomes work-up-gated, amiloride as the escalation step (nephrologist section 0). 3 becomes the endocrinologist's diagnostic gate. 4 is conditional on aneuploid fraction in more than one tissue. 5 gains Tang 2011's metformin concentration as a condition. 6 is unmoved, its rejections resting on the error below.

## (c) Factual errors

1. Four files reject digoxin because a stabilised transcript still stops at 737 and yields no kinase domain. Dossier 01 sections 2.2 and 7: an engineered 731X truncation was functional in segregation assays when expressed, and catalysis is dispensable (PMID 22698286). The rejection survives on narrow index only.
2. CIN biologist: "the NMD premise survived computation (D6)". Creative D screens NMD-machinery genes; it measures nothing about this allele.
3. PK pharmacologist excludes metformin on AICAR's concentration. Tang 2011 ran a metformin arm.

## (d) The disagreement to keep

Position A, CIN biologist: relieving proteotoxic, energetic or lysosomal stress is tolerance delivered to the whole child, so chaperone inducers, phenylbutyrate and rapalogs are barred. Position B, mine: the lesion is BubR1 abundance, and raising it in every cell reduced aneuploidy and tumours (Baker 2013, PMID 23242215). Settling experiment: patient fibroblasts plus or minus a chaperone inducer, BubR1 immunoblot and interphase FISH aneuploid fraction at day 0 and 14. A predicts both rise, B predicts BubR1 up and aneuploid fraction down.

## (e) Cross-checks

**(1) Metformin PK.** The gap excludes the AICAR-equivalence argument, not the drug. The deciding number is metformin's own effective concentration in Tang 2011, which no file quotes and must be read from PMC3532042 first. Pallister-Killian does not close it: preprint, AICAR, no metformin arm.

**(2) The single RT-PCR.** It works, with conditions.

- Material: 2.5 mL blood in a PAXgene RNA tube, plus a paired PHA-stimulated lymphocyte or LCL culture split into vehicle and cycloheximide 100 micrograms/mL for 4 to 6 h, which turns "the allele is absent" into "absent through NMD".
- Primers: forward in exon 16 or the 5' half of exon 17 (about c.2050 to c.2150), reverse in exon 23 (about c.3150 to c.3250), Tm 60 C, cDNA product 950 to 1,150 bp spanning six exon junctions, so genomic template cannot amplify.
- Platform: barcoded amplicon sequencing on ONT or PacBio HiFi, each read crossing both sites; 500 consensus reads give a haplotype fraction to about two percentage points.
- Controls: both parents as single-allele controls, three age-matched controls, a synthetic 1:1 template mix for amplification bias.
- Turnaround: 3 to 5 working days from RNA where ONT runs, 2 weeks in a service lab, longer if a line must be grown.
- Cost: about 30 EUR per sample for extraction, 15 for RT and long PCR, 60 to 90 for a shared run; under 1,000 EUR for the whole set.

Outcomes. Opposite haplotypes: trans confirmed. Same haplotype: cis, and the panel's premise collapses. c.2210 under 10% of reads, restored by cycloheximide: NMD efficient, readthrough has no substrate, T4 becomes decisive. c.2210 at 30 to 50%: NMD inefficient, look for an 82 kDa product and digoxin is redundant. Balanced transcript, no protein: the deficit is translational.

Failure modes. No stored RNA and no fibroblast line means a fresh draw, consent and a visit, which the family representative is right to count. BUB1B is a proliferation gene, so resting blood may express too little to amplify: hence the stimulated arm, and buccal or urine RNA is no substitute. PCR bias over a 900 bp amplicon distorts the ratio, so without the controls the number is not quantitative. Cycloheximide blocks translation broadly, so the NMD comparison is qualitative. And the result changes no prescription this month; it re-ranks hypotheses.

**(3) Rule above drug.** Yes: at 5% added harm and a 20 to 30% residual share a drug needs a 22 to 64% relative cancer reduction to break even, none has a measured effect on cancer incidence in MVA, and a rule carries no added-harm term. The carve-out must be positive and sit above the list: "This page restricts elective and non-oncological prescribing only. It does not modify any oncology protocol. Vincristine, vinorelbine, dactinomycin, ifosfamide, cyclophosphamide and every other agent given with curative, relapse or disease-control intent are prescribed at protocol dose and schedule. No omission or substitution is to be made on the basis of this page."

**(4) The 10% negative.** Not a premise in either direction. Creative F.2 bounds a single clonal trisomy at about 4.2% on large autosomes and 11.7% per 5 Mb bin genome-wide corrected; a variegated burden over five chromosomes needs about 21% to be visible, over twenty-two it is undetectable, and a tissue-confined clone has sensitivity zero. The premise must come from interphase FISH or PCS karyotype in more than one tissue. Until then hydroxychloroquine and metformin address a compartment of unknown size.
