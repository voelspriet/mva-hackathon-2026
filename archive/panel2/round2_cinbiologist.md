# Round 2, chromosomal instability biologist (panel 2)

## (a) Objections to the other seven

**PK pharmacologist.** Thiazide plus citrate is ranked on exposure certainty, a property of the measurement rather than of the disease; by that criterion any urinary agent outranks every mechanism-directed one. Second claim: the metformin exclusion "on arithmetic" sets metformin plasma (8 to 15 micromolar) against AICAR's effective concentration (200 to 500 micromolar). Those are two compounds, and Tang 2011 gives no concentration for metformin (PMID 21315436, PMC3532042).

**Lab geneticist.** The aneugen rule ranks first while section 4 concedes the sign may be inverted (Sudo 2004, PMID 15059905), justified as surviving "every unmeasured fact except T1"; untestability is not evidence. Second claim: T2, that one cDNA amplicon returns phase and NMD efficiency together; see (e2).

**Predisposition oncologist.** The avoid rule is called "the only item with positive expected value under the F4 thresholds", but F4 is arithmetic on cancer-risk reduction and no avoidance rule has a measured effect on incidence either (dossier 02 section 6). Second claim: metformin is tiered "human patient cells" on the Pallister-Killian result, which used AICAR (creative G5).

**Immunologist.** The mTOR exclusion is right on the wrong ground: no NK or T cell measurement exists in any MVA patient, as the file opens by saying, whereas rapamycin improving aneuploid yeast fitness (dossier 02 section 5) carries the bar without an unmeasured premise. Second claim: item 1 protects clearance while item 6 proposes baricitinib, which removes IL-15-driven NK maturation.

**Nephrologist.** Its own citation says 61 to 90 percent of preterm nephrocalcinosis resolves by 12 to 24 months (PMID 16705005), which argues for the gate rather than for ranking the drug pair first. Second claim: glycerol over sodium phenylbutyrate settles a salt question and imports a chemical chaperone, the aneuploidy-tolerating direction (Donnelly 2014, PMID 25205676).

**Endocrinologist.** Section 4 concedes the GH refusal rests on inference and that posterior fossa malformation in a quarter of MVA1 leaves a treatable lesion open, so it is a gate, not a first-ranked bar. Second claim: metformin as "a net negative from the growth side" assumes appetite suppression at a dose never given for this purpose.

**Family representative.** Thiazide plus citrate is now first in four of eight files, turning the panel's output into nephrology for a checkpoint disorder. Second claim: the rule that cancer-risk arguments cannot buy harm excludes in advance the only class that could reduce cancer risk. I adopt the warning that promising such reduction in a lay summary is this report's most likely harm.

## (b) Revised ranking

1. Merged avoidance rule: aneugens, tolerance agents (chaperone inducers, chemical chaperones, rapalogs) and CDK4/6 inhibitors, carve-out at the head. Up from 2, per (e3).
2. Metformin, conditional on an ex vivo concentration-response at or below 20 micromolar. Down from 1, per (e1).
3. No growth hormone, no mecasermin, held as a gate rather than a ban, per the endocrinologist.
4. No immunomodulator without a measured interferon score, NK count and micronucleus frequency, from the immunologist's item 2.
5. Hydroxychloroquine, ex vivo only. Down, on the TLR7 and TLR9 antigen-presentation point and the monitoring cost.
6. Cystatin C for every renal threshold, from the nephrologist and pharmacologist, since it sets the stop rules on 2 and 5.

## (d) The disagreement not to paper over

Position A, the PK pharmacologist with the lab geneticist adjacent: no aneuploidy-selective candidate reaches its effective concentration at a defensible paediatric dose, so the honest output is supportive care plus rules. Position B, me with the oncologist and the family representative: metformin is the only approved agent with a published direction of effect against aneuploid cells, and the exposure objection compares the wrong two numbers. Settled by a metformin concentration-response, 1 to 50 micromolar, on this child's fibroblasts and the Pallister-Killian line, aneuploid versus euploid fraction by FISH over four weeks, AICAR as control, threshold declared first. A null at 20 micromolar settles it for A.

## (e) Cross-checks

**(1) Exposure.** It does not exclude metformin, since AICAR's 200 to 500 micromolar is AICAR's number. Two things do count against it and were not raised: uptake needs OCT1 or OCT3, poorly expressed in fibroblasts, so an ex vivo null could be a transport artefact; and metformin activates AMPK indirectly, which the authors name as the likely reason it was weaker. The Pallister-Killian result cannot rescue it, having used AICAR. The burden shifts: metformin is not rankable as a drug to give until a concentration-response exists.

**(2) The single RT-PCR.** It works for allele-specific expression and NMD efficiency, and returns phase only where NMD has failed: efficient decay removes the c.2210 allele from cDNA, and that absence is the NMD answer, not a phase answer. A parallel reaction under NMD inhibition, or phase read from genomic DNA at single-molecule length, separates them, at a cost trivial beside the WGS already done. Cis voids the panel. Trans with efficient decay kills the readthrough arm and leaves my selection-directed items untouched, since they do not depend on residual protein. Trans with escape revives that arm above my rank 2.

**(3) Rule above drug.** Yes, when the drug's break-even is unmet and the rule costs a bounded substitution. Under F4, at a residual risk share of 20 percent with 5 percent added harm, a drug must cut cancer risk by 64 percent and none has any measured effect; the rule costs terbinafine instead of griseofulvin. The carve-out goes in the first paragraph: "This list governs elective, non-oncological prescribing only. It does not modify any curative-intent or relapse protocol. Vincristine, vinorelbine, dactinomycin, cyclophosphamide and ifosfamide are not on this list and must not be omitted, substituted or dose-reduced on the basis of this document. No hypersensitivity to any spindle agent has been demonstrated in MVA patient cells (dossier 01, section 4.3)."

**(4) The 10 percent premise.** No candidate may rest on it. It is a per-segment bound under genome-wide correction; per chromosome the bound is about 4.2 percent on large autosomes, a burden over five chromosomes needs about 21 percent total to be visible, over 22 it is undetectable, and a tissue-confined clone is invisible at any fraction (creative F2). It neither establishes a target nor rules one out, and must not be quoted as reassurance either way. FISH or single-cell sequencing on blood, buccal cells and skin replaces it, and this gap now gates my rank 2 as well as my rank 5.
