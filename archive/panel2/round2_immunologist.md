**Family representative.** No objection to HCTZ plus citrate. Second claim: rejecting J2 because "the cleared compartment is the muscle and fat the child cannot spare" treats raising clearance and protecting it as one act. My item 1 removes no cells.

**Lab geneticist.** Aneugen avoidance is ranked first in the table without the carve-out section 4 then calls mandatory; the two belong in one sentence. Second claim: T2's single RT-PCR, see (e)(2).

**Nephrologist.** The top item is ranked as a drug, but section 4 states 61 to 90 percent of preterm nephrocalcinosis resolves untreated (PMID 16705005), which makes the work-up the ranked item and the drug conditional. ## (c) Factual errors

CIN biologist section 2 cites Bakhoum 2018 (PMID 29342134) for immune clearance constraining aneuploid clones; the paper reports chronic cGAS-STING signalling promoting metastasis, the opposite sign. Family representative section 3: "Sirolimus and everolimus, boxed malignancy and infection warnings". Sirolimus carries that boxed warning; everolimus as Afinitor and Votubia does not.

## (d) The disagreement not to paper over

Position A (CIN biologist, adopted by the oncologist and family representative): select against the aneuploid compartment pharmacologically; interferon and NK measurement is background. Position B (mine): that compartment is already under immune selection, so the move is protecting and measuring it, and any drug relieving aneuploid stress or suppressing clearance is a harm. They rule oppositely on hydroxychloroquine, which blocks autophagy in aneuploid cells and antigen presentation at one dose. Settling experiment: on one draw, interferon score, NK count and CD107a, and interphase FISH aneuploid fraction, in the child and both carrier parents; then an NK killing assay on patient fibroblasts with aneuploid and euploid nuclei scored by FISH, plus or minus hydroxychloroquine at 1 to 10 micromolar. No change in aneuploid fraction under NK co-culture supports A; a kill that hydroxychloroquine blunts supports B.

## (e) Cross-checks

**(1) Metformin PK.** The gap excludes metformin as a copy of AICAR at those doses, not as a candidate: Tang 2011 tested metformin itself and reported impaired accumulation of trisomy 13 and 16 cells, so the relevant number is metformin's own effective concentration, which that paper does not give. The Pallister-Killian result used AICAR and its metformin arm was never run, so it does not close the gap. Verdict: demoted to an ex vivo arm, not excluded. It clears my item 1 filter, being non-immunosuppressive.

**(2) The single RT-PCR.** It returns both answers only if the c.2210 transcript reaches the cDNA. Under efficient decay the null allele is depleted from the amplicon, phase cannot be called there, and the depletion is itself the NMD measurement, so the assay silently becomes a one-answer test. Fix: long-read gDNA for phase, the cDNA amplicon for allele ratio, and a puromycin or cycloheximide arm to restore the null transcript. Cost: one RNA sample, one reverse transcription, one long amplicon, single-molecule sequencing; low hundreds of euros plus the sampling visit. Outcomes: trans with efficient decay confirms loss of dosage and kills the NMD-stabilisation pairing; trans with inefficient decay plus a detectable truncate promotes the lab geneticist's rank 6; cis voids every list here.

**(3) Rules above drugs.** Yes: each rule rests on human label or genotoxicity data at no added exposure, while each drug rests on cell or mouse tiers. Carve-out wording, same page, same type: "This list governs elective, non-oncological prescribing only. It does not modify, delay, substitute or de-escalate any curative-intent cancer protocol, including vincristine, vinorelbine and dactinomycin, and no agent may be omitted from such a protocol on the basis of this list." Each entry names one agent and its substitute, never a class.

**(4) The 10 percent premise.** It cannot carry an aneuploidy-selective candidate. A burden spread over five chromosomes is bounded only near 21 percent in blood, and a clone confined to kidney or muscle is unbounded (creative F section 2), so the target is neither shown to exist nor bounded above. It cuts my way too: clearance may be doing work no blood measurement in hand would show. Interphase FISH or single-cell sequencing per tissue precedes any candidate claiming to act on aneuploid cells.
