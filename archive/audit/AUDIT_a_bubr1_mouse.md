# AUDIT (a): BubR1 molecular biology and mouse models

Partition (a) of `PROMPT_A_source_audit.md`. Scope audited: every claim in the deliverables about
BubR1 protein structure and domains, the pseudokinase question, MVA patient cell lines and their
protein or mRNA levels, the BubR1 mouse allelic series and its lifespans and phenotypes, the p16,
INK-ATTAC and SIRT2 crosses, BubR1 overexpression, and residue numbering between species. Claims
belonging to clinical oncology, nephrology, pharmacology, statistics, genetics databases and
aneuploidy immunology are out of scope and were not audited.

Claims already adjudicated in V1, V2 and V3 were re-derived from source rather than carried across.
