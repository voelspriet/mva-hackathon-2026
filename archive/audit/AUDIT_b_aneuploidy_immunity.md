# AUDIT b: aneuploidy biology, senescence and immune surveillance

Partition (b) of PROMPT_A_source_audit.md. Scope: every claim in the deliverables about aneuploidy-selective compounds (Tang 2011 and its concentrations for AICAR, metformin, chloroquine and 17-AAG), proteotoxic and lysosomal stress in aneuploid cells, autophagy dependence, HSP90 and aneuploidy tolerance, cGAS-STING and micronuclei, NK and immune clearance of aneuploid or senescent cells, p16-driven senescence as a mechanism, and the direction of effect of any of these on tumour risk. Claims belonging to other partitions were not audited.

## Two notes that are not claim verdicts

1. **Omission bearing on the load-bearing disagreement.** The deliverables treat the tumour direction of senescence in this gene as unresolved (REPORT_track2.md:201, :271, :306). Baker 2008, already cited in the same report for the ageing half, contains a direct tumour result in the same mouse: "BubR1 H/H ; p16 Ink4a −/− mice had significantly more tumours than BubR1 H/H mice (Fig. 8a)", with eight of nine being lung adenocarcinomas against one in BubR1^H/H and none in p16^Ink4a−/−. Baker 2008 also reports that p19Arf loss and BubR1 insufficiency "do not synergize in tumorigenesis". This is genetic rather than pharmacological evidence, so it does not close the senolytic-drug question, and it should be printed where the direction is discussed.

2. **Version divergence in the Tang 2011 supplement.** The published supplement and the NIHMS author manuscript number the Figure S1 panels differently. Beclin-1 knockdown is panel B in the manuscript quoted by V1 and panel E in the published version, and the published legend also names 2-deoxyglucose. Any citation to a Tang 2011 supplemental panel letter should state which version it reads.
