# Prompt: divergent ideas for Track 2 before synthesis

## Why this round exists

The five-member panel converged on nephrocalcinosis care, a measurement gate, and three aneuploidy- or senescence-directed drugs, and excluded most other classes. Convergence after two rounds can mean the frame was narrow. This round asks for ideas outside that frame, generated without reading the panel files.

## Facts (same as archive/PROMPT_track2.md)

BUB1B compound heterozygous: c.2210T>G p.Leu737Ter (exon 17 of 23, predicted NMD substrate, no truncated protein measured) and c.3006T>G p.Asn1002Lys (exon 23 of 23, kinase domain, six residues from PLK1 site T1008, VUS, no functional data). Phase unproven. Mosaic aneuploidy not detected above about 10% of cells in the sequenced sample. Phenotype: rhabdomyosarcoma (treated), nephrocalcinosis, short stature, failure to thrive, low muscle mass, born at 32 weeks small for gestational age; parental recurrent miscarriage. Scope: market-approved medicines (any jurisdiction, state which), combinations allowed, supplements and investigational compounds may be mentioned only as context. Judged on rigor, impact, innovation, scalability.

## Directions to explore

Each direction is a question, not a conclusion. For every idea: the molecular or physiological step it acts on, the approved drug(s) with that action, the evidence tier (human, animal, cell, inference), the specific reason it might fail, and one measurement that would test it.

1. Allele-directed. The nonsense allele: which approved drugs promote translational readthrough of premature stop codons (for example ataluren, approved in the EU for nonsense-mutation Duchenne; aminoglycosides), what stop-codon context (UGA versus UAG versus UAA, and the +4 nucleotide) the L737* allele has, and what a readthrough product would be. Which approved drugs inhibit nonsense-mediated decay, and would a stabilised truncated transcript help or harm. The missense allele: if p.Asn1002Lys destabilises the protein, which approved drugs alter chaperone or proteasome activity in the direction of stabilisation, and what is the risk.
2. Expression-directed. BubR1 abundance is set by transcription (FOXM1, E2F), acetylation (PCAF) and deacetylation (SIRT2), and SUMOylation. Which approved drugs raise SIRT2 activity or NAD+ (prescription nicotinamide or niacin products, where approved), or alter histone deacetylation in a direction that raises BubR1, and what did the BubR1 acetylation-mimetic and SIRT2 mouse work show. Which approved drugs are known to lower BubR1 and must be avoided.
3. Segregation-directed. Which approved drugs at sub-therapeutic doses alter microtubule dynamics or kinetochore-microtubule attachment in a way that reduces, rather than increases, mis-segregation in checkpoint-weak cells (for example the paradox that very low-dose taxol can reduce lagging chromosomes in some CIN lines, and the opposite results). State the direction of each result.
4. Consequence-directed beyond senescence and autophagy. Aneuploid cells show altered metabolism, ER stress, immune visibility via cGAS-STING and micronuclei, and sphingolipid changes. Which approved drugs act on these, and which would be expected to worsen the state.
5. Tissue-directed. The child's burden is in muscle, growth and kidney. Which approved drugs used in sarcopenia, cachexia or failure to thrive (for example cyproheptadine, megestrol, oxandrolone, beta-agonists) are compatible with a cancer-predisposition syndrome and which are not, and why. Which approved drugs reduce nephrocalcinosis progression beyond thiazide and citrate.
6. Schedule-directed. Intermittent, pulsed, or exposure-limited regimens (hit-and-run senolysis, metronomic dosing) that change the risk profile of drugs the panel rejected on label warnings.
7. Analogies. Approved-drug repurposing that reached patients in another single-gene paediatric disease with a comparable mechanism gap (lonafarnib in progeria, ataluren in Duchenne, sirolimus in PROS, metformin in Fanconi anaemia): what was the argument that carried it, and does the same argument exist here.
8. Non-drug interventions that change what a drug can do: exercise in BubR1 mice, dietary NAD+ precursors, caloric restriction results in this model, and whether any is documented.
9. Trial-design ideas that make an N-of-1 result interpretable in an ultra-rare disease: parental carriers as within-family controls (heterozygous parents show PCS in lymphocytes), biomarker choice, crossover length, shared protocols across the roughly 40 known patients through the MVA Society.
