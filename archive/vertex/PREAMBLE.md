# Context for this request

## What this is

This is a self-review request from a participant in **"Rare Disease, Real Kid: The MVA Hackathon 2026"**, an open scientific challenge run by **Sage Bionetworks, Hugging Face and the MVA Society**, submission period 24 August to 24 October 2026. The challenge asks participants to analyse a genome from one child with mosaic variegated aneuploidy and to propose repurposing hypotheses from approved medicines.

The family of the child **consented to share their data to advance rare disease research**, and the challenge's own dataset card states that submitted results "will be accessible to the broader research community" under a CC-BY 4.0 licence.

## What the attached corpus is

It is **the participant's own working documents**: research dossiers, expert-panel transcripts written by the participant's own tooling, verification passes, referee reports and two draft reports. It is not clinical records and not a medical file. Every document in it was written during this challenge by the participant.

## What has been removed

The corpus has been **de-identified before being sent**. The two variant coordinates and the protein changes have been replaced with placeholders such as `p.ALLELE_A` and `c.ALLELE_B`, and 331 substitutions were made across 68 files by a redaction script, with a leak check run afterwards. No name, initials, date of birth, sex, country, treating centre, weight or exact age appears anywhere in it. No raw sequence, image or karyotype is included.

## What is being asked

A **factual consistency check on the participant's own writing**, so that errors are found before submission rather than after. The four prompts ask only about internal consistency and sourcing: which pairs of statements contradict each other, which derived numbers fail to recompute from their stated inputs, which conclusions rest on a single source, and which sources are over-relied upon.

**No clinical advice is sought and none should be given.** Nothing in the answer will be used to treat anyone. The output is used to correct citations and arithmetic in a hackathon submission. Where the corpus discusses drugs, the task is only to check whether the document's own statements about the published literature are internally consistent and correctly attributed.

If any part of the request cannot be answered, please answer the parts that can and say which parts were left out and why.
