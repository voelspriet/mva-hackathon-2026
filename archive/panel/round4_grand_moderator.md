## 3. Divergence between the teams

**Nephrology overturned team 1's unanimous rank 1.** All five team 1 files put potassium citrate with a conditional thiazide first, gated only on "urine chemistry". Team 2's nephrologist showed two causes demanding opposite prescriptions: nephrocalcinosis of prematurity, where 61 to 90 percent resolve by 12 to 24 months (PMID 19460127; 16705005), and ifosfamide proximal tubulopathy, where alkali and phosphate are the treatment (round1_nephrologist §0). The ranked item becomes the panel, not the drug, and amiloride enters as the escalation step (creative B3, J4).

**Endocrinology turned an exclusion into a gate.** Team 1 excluded growth hormone in one line; team 2 specified the re-entry route (IGF-1 and IGFBP-3 on stable intake, bone age, imaging if IGF-1 is low, Sklar 2018's one-disease-free-year condition, PMID 29982476) and added glucocorticoid sparing, an exposure team 1 never listed although dexamethasone accompanies every cycle. It also dismantled creative B6's growth-safety case for pulsed dasatinib: TKI growth deceleration is prepubertal with normal IGF-1, so it acts on cartilage and is not avoided by lowering occupancy (PMID 21592517). Team 1's cell biologist had argued the opposite (round2_cellbiologist §a).

**PK arithmetic reversed team 1's ranked list.** Metformin sat at median rank 4 in team 1. Team 2's PK seat computed plasma at 7.7 to 15.5 micromolar against AICAR at 200 to 500 micromolar and excluded it (round2_pkpharmacologist §e1); five of eight team 2 files then demoted it to an ex vivo pre-gate.

**Three further lenses.** CIN selection added a direction test team 1 never applied, scoring each candidate on whether it selects against the aneuploid compartment or delivers tolerance to the whole child, and barring chaperone inducers, rapalogs and the CDK4/6 inhibitors creative E surfaced (round1_cinbiologist rule 2). Immunology made clearance a scored axis and added vaccination with serology. Laboratory genetics collapsed team 1's three assays into one RT-PCR with an NMD-block arm (round2_labgeneticist §e2).

**What team 1 held and team 2 missed.** Team 1's oncologist ranked cyclophosphamide with mesna in place of ifosfamide second, median 3 across team 1; no team 2 seat proposed that substitution, although its own nephrologist named ifosfamide tubulopathy as a competing cause of the kidney lesion. Team 1 also kept dexrazoxane conditional on an anthracycline, where team 2's oncologist recorded that "nobody addressed it", and team 1 alone named the experiment settling the senolysis tumour-direction question.

## 5. Live disagreements after everything

B: team 1's oncologist, pharmacologist, family representative and geneticist, plus team 2's CIN biologist and predisposition oncologist, since mis-segregating cells arrest with senescence features and signalling that recruits clearance (PMID 28633018), p19Arf ablation worsened the same mouse, and tumour incidence was never measured. Settled by a senolytic given as a drug to BubR1^H/H or ^H/L1002P mice, tumour incidence co-primary, bone length secondary. Not done.

**2. Scope of the aneugen rule against curative chemotherapy.** A: team 2's predisposition oncologist with team 1's oncologist, since RMS 2005 maintenance carries 85.0 against 72.4 percent five-year overall survival (PMID 31562043); the rule governs elective prescribing only. B: team 2's CIN biologist ranks the rule "with a taxane and vinca flag" and bars "elective taxane or vinca", while team 1's geneticist, cell biologist and family representative ruled RMS chemotherapy out of scope entirely, which team 1's oncologist called a scope ruling presented as an evidence judgement. Settled by micronucleus frequency and apoptotic fraction under vincristine and vinorelbine at clinically achievable concentrations, in this child's stimulated lymphocytes and fibroblasts against both parents and three controls, blind, threshold pre-declared. Janssen 2009 (PMID 19855003) predicts amplified errors, Sudo 2004 (PMID 15059905) reduced killing, and no MVA cell has been tested (01 §4.3).

**3. Selection against aneuploid cells versus tissue reserve.** A: team 2's CIN biologist, on Tang 2011, Williams 2008 (PMID 18974345), Sheltzer 2017 (PMID 28089890) and the Pallister-Killian AICAR preprint (creative G lead 5). ## 6. The final output the report should carry

### (a) The measurement gate

1. **Tubular panel, one visit.** Serum and paired urine calcium, phosphate, bicarbonate, magnesium, creatinine, cystatin C, urine pH, low molecular weight protein, plus the neonatal furosemide record and cumulative ifosfamide dose. Decides disagreement 4, which alkali is correct, and the ceiling for every renally cleared drug.
2. **The single RT-PCR with an NMD-block arm.** Forward primer in exon 16 or the 5' half of exon 17 (about c.2050 to c.2150), reverse in exon 23 (about c.3150 to c.3250); cDNA product 950 to 1,150 bp across six exon junctions, so genomic template cannot amplify; single-molecule sequencing, 500 consensus reads; both parents, three controls, and a synthetic 1:1 template mix for amplification bias. The NMD-block condition is a paired stimulated lymphocyte or lymphoblastoid culture split into vehicle and cycloheximide 100 micrograms per mL for 4 to 6 h. Under 1,000 EUR, 3 to 5 working days where the sequencer is local. **Failure mode:** under efficient decay the c.2210 allele is depleted from the cDNA, so its absence mimics cis and the assay silently becomes a one-answer test, which is why the block arm, or long-read genomic phase, is not optional (round2_pkpharmacologist §e2; round2_immunologist §e2). It needs living cells, not the existing WGS. Decides phase, allele-specific expression and NMD efficiency together, and so whether the drug list has a premise.
3. **Aneuploid fraction per tissue:** FISH or single-cell sequencing on blood, buccal cells and skin, plus a PCS karyotype, child and both parents. Decides disagreement 3 and whether any aneuploidy-selective candidate has a target.
4. **BubR1 immunoblot** as a percentage of control against both parents and three controls. Decides which consequence dominates.
5. **Cycloheximide chase on p.Asn1002Lys,** Suijkerbuijk 2010 design. Decides its functional class and the precondition for disagreement 5.
6. **Endocrine gate:** IGF-1 and IGFBP-3 SDS on stable intake, bone age, thyroid function, morning cortisol, imaging if IGF-1 is low. Decides the growth hormone re-entry route.
7. **Interferon score (PMID 24183309), immunophenotype, immunoglobulins and vaccine titres,** child and both parents. Decides whether any immunomodulator is a candidate.
8. **p16 in patient tissue.** Decides whether the senescence hypothesis has a human baseline.

### (b) The avoidance rule with its carve-out

Taken from the predisposition oncologist (round2_predispositiononcologist §e3), the best of the seven drafted, because it separates scope from application and names substitutes agent by agent; the acute-indication clause is the nephrologist's (round2_nephrologist §e3), and the rule that no chemotherapy agent appears in the barred column is family representative 2's (round2_family2 §e3).

> **SCOPE.** Elective, non-oncological prescribing only.
>
> **DOES NOT APPLY TO** any medicine given as part of a curative-intent, relapse or protocol-defined cancer treatment plan. Vincristine, vinorelbine, dactinomycin, irinotecan, temozolomide, cyclophosphamide and ifosfamide are not restricted by this page. Do not reduce, omit, delay or substitute any agent in a rhabdomyosarcoma protocol on the basis of this page. EpSSG RMS 2005 maintenance gives 5-year overall survival of 85.0 percent against 72.4 percent (PMID 31562043). No study has shown MVA or BUB1B patient cells to be hypersensitive to any spindle agent: the sensitisation data come from engineered knockdown tumour cells (PMID 19855003), and the opposing data show BUB1B suppression producing taxane resistance (PMID 15059905). It also does not withhold a loop diuretic, corticosteroid, antibiotic or antiemetic given for an acute indication where no equivalent alternative exists. Decisions on a cancer regimen belong to the treating oncologist and the tumour board.
>
> **APPLIES TO** colchicine, griseofulvin, albendazole, mebendazole, acetazolamide, topiramate, elective loop diuretics, high-dose intermittent vitamin D, somatotropin, mecasermin. Substitutes: terbinafine; an NSAID or anakinra; levetiracetam; a thiazide; daily vitamin D to a measured 25-OH-D; for growth, the endocrine gate.

Each carries the label **hypothesis for follow-up**, in the hackathon's own words.

1. **The avoidance and substitution rule with the carve-out.** Tier human genotoxicity and label data per agent, inference for the transfer; condition the carve-out in the same type size; harm over-application withholding curative chemotherapy; readout avoid-list agents prescribed per patient-year through the UK MVA audit, not compliance. First in four of eight team 2 round-1 files.
2. **Tubular work-up, then potassium citrate; thiazide only on documented hypercalciuria; amiloride as escalation.** Tier human, other paediatric hypercalciuric disease (PMID 11433085); condition gate (a)1, urine pH below 7.5, thiazide held through any cytotoxic block; harm hypokalaemia, volume depletion, hypercalcaemia; readout urine calcium to creatinine, citrate, potassium, bicarbonate, ultrasound over two years.
3. **Cyproheptadine, after a food-first run-in of 8 to 12 weeks.** Tier human, small uncontrolled paediatric series (PMID 18989154; 24941960); condition a body-composition readout; harm sedation, rare hepatotoxicity, weight gained as fat; readout MUAC z-score monthly, lean mass index at 0 and 12 weeks.
4. **Vaccination completed with serology; immunoglobulin only on a documented deficit.** Tier human in other populations, inference for MVA; condition panel (a)7, live vaccines withheld under any immunosuppressant; harm false reassurance if titres go unchecked; readout protective titres at 4 to 6 weeks, infection days per year.
5. **Cyclophosphamide with mesna for ifosfamide, and dexrazoxane with an anthracycline, both relapse-conditional.** Tier human other for the regimens, BubR1 mouse for cardiac organ overlap only; harm haemorrhagic cystitis, gonadal toxicity, alkylator second malignancy, and for dexrazoxane a secondary-malignancy label flag (creative E); readout tubular handling, strain and troponin.
6. **Metformin, ex vivo pre-gate only, not a prescription.** Tier cell, mouse MEFs at 0.5 to 1.0 mM (V1 §1b); condition a concentration-response in patient or Pallister-Killian fibroblasts at 5, 15, 50 and 500 micromolar with AICAR control over three to four passages, threshold filed first; harm lactic acidosis with falling filtration, appetite and weight loss; readout aneuploid fraction by FISH.
7. **Hydroxychloroquine, ex vivo gated, time-limited, never chronic.** Tier cell, and with chloroquine rather than hydroxychloroquine, called the weakest of three and without effect on human tumour lines (V1 §1c); condition a measured aneuploid fraction and an achievable retinal screening plan; harm retinopathy against a syndromic eye phenotype, QT over thiazide hypokalaemia, antigen-presentation blockade; readout aneuploid fraction by FISH.

Unresolved, not ranked: **intermittent dasatinib as a senolytic**, held open only because disagreement 1 is unsettled.

### (d) Explicitly not recommended

Growth hormone and mecasermin outside the endocrine gate (no MVA1 efficacy data, no response in the two MVA2 patients tested, PMID 36588761, labelled contraindication with a malignancy history); rapalogs (immunosuppression against clearance, proteinuria, and a direction that may relieve rather than exploit aneuploid stress); chaperone inducers and phenylbutyrate (pending disagreement 5); CDK4/6 inhibitors (no MVA evidence, marrow and growth-plate effects); hydroxyurea (it raises micronucleated reticulocytes, the readout); acetazolamide, topiramate and elective loop diuretics (hypercalciuria into an established lesion); colchicine, griseofulvin, albendazole, mebendazole (approved aneugens); systemic aminoglycosides (nephrotoxicity in the organ under treatment, whatever the clean MT-RNR1 result, creative D §6); ataluren (unobtainable outside Great Britain, poor codon context); digoxin (refused on therapeutic index, not on mechanism, after the V1 correction); proteasome inhibitors (they block mitotic exit); HSP90 inhibitors including pimitespib (they accelerate turnover of the only BubR1 this child makes); low-dose paclitaxel (the measured direction in this genotype class is sensitisation); and every non-approved compound and supplement, quercetin, fisetin, NMN, nicotinamide riboside, navitoclax, AICAR, 17-AAG and the Mps1, Aurora B, PLK1 and KIF18A inhibitors included.

## 7. Endpoints

Renal entries: urine calcium to creatinine, citrate, pH, potassium and bicarbonate, with the ultrasound trajectory over two years rather than six weeks, since 61 to 90 percent of preterm nephrocalcinosis resolves untreated by 24 months (PMID 16705005). Aneuploidy-selective entries: FISH or single-cell sequencing, per tissue. Nutrition: MUAC and lean mass index. Immunology: titres and infection days. Relapse entries: tubular handling, strain and troponin. Senolysis if pursued: p16 in sorted T cells with a muscle-function measure and one parent-observable number.

Rejected. Micronucleus frequency as primary endpoint for aneuploidy-selective agents, on two grounds both teams reached: it scores ongoing mis-segregation while chloroquine's activity was selective killing of cells already aneuploid, so a fall is not predicted and its absence would falsify nothing; and with no baseline here a pre-specified 30 percent fall may sit inside OECD TG 487 variance. It stays as baseline and stopping rule. Height velocity and bone age at 6 and 12 months as a growth-safety readout: too slow, replaced by knemometry at 0.09 to 0.16 mm resolution over 2 to 4 weeks (creative J6, PMID 26854116). Family-collected options kept: buccal micronucleus cytome, posted and blind-scored (J1); home urine calcium to creatinine strips (J13); grip strength and a timed five-rise test (J14); the three-day diary (H §5). Family-collected options rejected: parents dosed in vivo as written (C1, J3), needing their own protocol and consent, and whole-body MRI, since no validated imaging surveillance protocol for MVA exists.

Power, from creative F §3 and §5. FISH detects a 22 percent change at p0 = 0.10 with 1,000 nuclei and three measurements per period, against 41 percent for micronuclei at an elevated baseline; a flow reticulocyte readout counting 1e6 cells drives scoring noise to CV 0.022 and still lands at 37.5 percent, so the three-per-period design, not the assay, binds. Periods follow pool turnover: 12 weeks for lymphocyte endpoints plus a 4-week washout, about 11 months in total, or 4-week periods on a reticulocyte readout. A parallel randomised design is out of reach below about a 50 percent effect, while an aggregated crossover reaches a 20 percent change with 17 subjects, inside an enrollable population of roughly 15 to 20.

## 10. Honest self-assessment

**What this can claim.** A documented, citable procedure, run twice by panels that did not read each other, with a verification pass that changed four positions they held. It transfers to another chromosomal instability disorder with the gene and partner set swapped (creative E, transfer section).

**What it cannot claim.** Nothing here is evidence that a medicine works. No candidate has been tested in an MVA patient or in MVA patient cells, and no case report exists of any repurposed agent given to an MVA patient for the syndrome (creative G §7). No approved drug has a measured effect on cancer incidence in this disease, and there are zero registered interventional trials (02 §6). Phase is unproven, residual BubR1 unmeasured, and the missense allele a variant of uncertain significance.

**What a critic attacks first.** That the two top-ranked outputs are a prescribing rule and a work-up, so a repurposing brief has been answered largely by declining to prescribe; five of thirteen seats said as much in their own strongest-objection sections. Then that independence is partial: both teams read the same five dossiers, so convergence measures dossier agreement as much as expert agreement, and inputs were asymmetric because only team 2 saw the creative round. Then that the Baker 2013 and Wu 2023 rows rest on abstracts. Then that a panel of assigned roles reproduces the caution its brief rewards.

**Where the panels may share a blind spot.** No member of either team found an error inside a dossier; every correction concerned a reading of one (round3_moderator §4). Both reasoned from a mouse hypomorph whose allelic identity differs from this child's, although Sieben 2020 (PMID 31738183) is the paper showing that allelic identity, not total level, drives outcome. Both worked without the child's age, weight, current medications or whether ifosfamide was given, and every renal, PK and dosing conclusion turns on that last item. Both accepted p.Leu737Ter as pathogenic while the dossier records 115 heterozygotes at gnomAD v4 AF 7.87e-5 and no published MVA patient carrying it (01 §2.4). Neither team asked whether the diagnosis is right. One discrepancy no member raised: archive/PROMPT_track2.md gives c.2209T>G where 01 §2.4 gives c.2210T>G at the same coordinate.
