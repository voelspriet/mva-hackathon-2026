## 2. Overreach

**§2, "Independent convergence is the strongest signal in this exercise."** §10 concedes both teams read the same five dossiers. That qualifier belongs at first use: agreement on a point traceable to a dossier is weaker evidence than agreement on one neither dossier states.

**§6(c) item 6, the metformin ladder "at 5, 15, 50 and 500 micromolar".** V1 §1b gives 0.5 and 1.0 mM as the only concentrations tested, an effect the authors call "subtle" (Ts13 73±2.9 against WT 86±2.4), and no IC50. A ladder whose top rung is the bottom of the only effective range cannot return an interpretable negative. Required: a 1.0 mM arm, and a pre-declaration that no effect below 500 micromolar is predicted and does not refute the mechanism.

## 3. Omission

**No cardiac baseline in the measurement gate (§6(a), eight items).** BubR1 hypomorphs died of cardiac failure after senescent-cell clearance improved every other measured outcome (PMID 22048312); BubR1^H/L1002P shows low cardiac stress tolerance and an ECG J-point defect, the one endpoint SIRT2 reversed (PMID 24825348; dossier 01 §3, §4.1). I added echocardiography in round2_cellbiologist §b; it did not survive, and §7 assigns strain and troponin to relapse entries only. An ECG and baseline echo are cheap, and they set the ceiling on what any healthspan-directed intervention can claim.

**The expression-directed arm has no landing place.** §4 records that V2 §2 killed the premise used to reject niacin, acipimox and nicotinamide, and says this "revives the expression-directed arm". Nothing in §6(c), §6(d) or §9 carries it, though both are approved medicines. A verification pass that overturns an exclusion must restore the candidate or state the surviving reason to exclude it, here the weak NAD+ to BubR1 link and V2 §3's sex-specific, marginal result (P=0.0384) in one progeroid model.

## 4. The verified facts

**Metformin.** V1 §1b changes my position: I ranked it 3 at round 2 on the argument that its tier equalled hydroxychloroquine's. Against paediatric plasma of 7.7 to 15.5 micromolar the gap is 32 to 130 fold, for an effect the authors call subtle. Not a prescribing candidate at any rank; §6(c) item 6, ex vivo pre-gate only, is correct and I adopt it.

**731X.** V1 §2a changes more. I wrote in round 1 that a real fix is protein restoration, treating the null allele as dead ground. The 731X protein rescues checkpoint, alignment and unperturbed segregation fidelity and sits at 1.44 times wild-type expression once NMD is bypassed (PMID 20516114, Figures 5A-5C, and 3D of the 2012 reuse). For allele 1 the therapeutic object is the transcript, not the fold. Three boundaries stand: no construct terminating at 737 was tested, all of it is HeLa and U2OS replacement rather than an organism, and no approved agent stabilises this transcript at an acceptable therapeutic index. It adds no drug, but it turns gate (a)2 from a diagnostic into the precondition for a class of hypothesis. Say so where that gate is described.

## 5. Sign-off

§6 represents my position. Dasatinib should be printed as §6(c) prints it: not a candidate, not ranked, named as the unresolved question with its experiment. My round-2 defence of pulsing is refuted on its own terms by team 2's endocrinologist: TKI growth deceleration is prepubertal with normal IGF-1 and IGFBP-3, so it acts on cartilage, which lowering occupancy does not avoid (PMID 21592517). Team 2's immunologist adds that dasatinib blocks TCR and NK activating signalling within hours, suppressing the clearance arm my own objection says may be restraining the aneuploid compartment. With the direction open on three independent routes and every schedule refuted, a drug name inside a ranked list functions as a candidate whatever label sits beside it.
