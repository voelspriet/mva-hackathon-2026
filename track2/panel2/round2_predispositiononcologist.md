Line 15: CDK4/6 inhibitors do not "license aneuploid cells to keep dividing"; they enforce G1 arrest, and trilaciclib is used for deliberate arrest (creative E).

**Lab geneticist.** Aneugen avoidance at rank 1: your section 4 says the sign may be wrong (Sudo 2004, PMID 15059905), yet the table names the harm as "over-application withholding curative chemotherapy" without drafting the exclusion that prevents it. **PK pharmacologist.** Thiazide plus citrate at rank 1: exposure certainty is not benefit certainty, and the nephrologist's PMID 16705005 has 61 to 90 percent of preterm nephrocalcinosis resolving untreated by 24 months. Your metformin exclusion uses the wrong comparator, see (e)(1).

## (c) Factual errors

CIN biologist line 25 rejects niacin, acipimox and nicotinamide because "raising BubR1 in every cell also raises aneuploid fitness". Inverted: sustained high BubR1 reduces aneuploidy and tumorigenesis, including against oncogenic Ras, and extends lifespan (Baker 2013, PMID 23242215; dossier 01 section 4.1). Reject them on the weak NAD+ to BubR1 link instead. Line 15, as above, states the opposite of the CDK4/6 mechanism in creative E.

## (d) The disagreement not to paper over

Position A, mine, with the lab geneticist's section 4 and the family representative's rejection of B10: the aneugen rule covers elective non-oncological prescribing only, and vincas and dactinomycin are protected inside curative-intent protocols.

Position B, the CIN biologist: rank 3 is "aneugen avoidance, with a taxane and vinca flag", and the exclusion list carries "elective taxane or vinca". A flag on a class has no defined behaviour at a relapse tumour board, and that is how a regimen gets de-escalated.

Settled by: micronucleus frequency and apoptotic fraction, dose-response to vincristine and vinorelbine at clinically achievable concentrations, in this child's stimulated lymphocytes and fibroblasts against both parents and three controls, blind, threshold pre-declared. Sudo 2004 and the CRISPR screen (dossier 02 section 4) predict reduced killing, Janssen 2009 amplified errors; neither has been run in MVA cells (dossier 01 section 4.3).

## (e) Cross-checks

**(1) Metformin exposure.** It demotes, it does not exclude. Plasma metformin against AICAR's 0.2 to 0.5 mM compares two molecules on two potency scales; Tang 2011 tested metformin itself, so read its own effective concentration off that paper. The Pallister-Killian result also changes the test: a selection assay over serial passage, not acute cytotoxicity, so a small per-division fitness differential compounds and a concentration far below any acute EC50 can shift clone fraction over weeks. OCT1 partitioning does not apply to fibroblasts. Verdict: a pre-gate, patient or carrier fibroblasts at 8 to 15 micromolar over three to four passages, aneuploid fraction by FISH, threshold declared first. Intracellular-to-plasma ratio in parenchyma is the pharmacologist's question.

**(2) The single RT-PCR.** Works for allele ratio, conditionally for phase. Both sites sit on one 796 nt cDNA amplicon, inside a single long read or a 2x300 paired read, so co-occurrence reads per molecule. The failure mode matters: if NMD is efficient the c.2210 allele contributes few molecules, and absence of a molecule carrying both changes cannot separate cis from decay. Fix: run the amplicon with and without an NMD block, since under block both alleles are represented and phase reads directly. Cost: one culture, a cycloheximide arm, one RT, one PCR, amplicon sequencing; days, consumables in the low hundreds. Outcomes: under-representation restored by block confirms NMD and keeps the readthrough arm closed; near-parity without block means inefficient NMD, so A1 and A3 reopen behind a western for a band near 82 kDa; both changes on one molecule under block means cis, voiding every list here except the avoid rule and the nephrology items. It substitutes for a trio when parents are unavailable, and needs living cells, so not from the existing WGS.

**(3) Rule above drug, and the wording.** Yes, when the arithmetic governing the drugs is unfavourable: a rule has no exposure, dose or monitoring cost, so its expected value is positive at any nonzero probability that the class effect transfers. It cannot be falsified, and it can be over-applied; the second is fixed by wording. Page text:

> **SCOPE.** Elective, non-oncological prescribing only.
>
> **DOES NOT APPLY TO** any medicine given as part of a curative-intent or protocol-defined cancer treatment plan. Vincristine, vinorelbine, dactinomycin, irinotecan, temozolomide, cyclophosphamide and ifosfamide are NOT restricted by this page. Do not reduce, omit, delay or substitute any agent in a rhabdomyosarcoma protocol on the basis of this page. EpSSG RMS 2005 maintenance gives 5-year overall survival of 85.0 percent with vinorelbine and cyclophosphamide against 72.4 percent without (Bisogno 2019, PMID 31562043). No study has shown MVA or BUB1B patient cells to be hypersensitive to any spindle agent: the sensitisation data are from engineered BUB1B-knockdown tumour cells (Janssen 2009, PMID 19855003), and the opposing data show BUB1B suppression producing taxane resistance (Sudo 2004, PMID 15059905). Decisions on a cancer regimen belong to the treating oncologist and the tumour board, not to this page.
>
> **APPLIES TO** colchicine, griseofulvin, albendazole, mebendazole, acetazolamide, topiramate, loop diuretics, high-dose intermittent (stoss) vitamin D, somatotropin, mecasermin. Substitutes: terbinafine; an NSAID or anakinra; levetiracetam; a thiazide; daily vitamin D titrated to a measured 25-OH-D.

**(4) The 10 percent premise.** It carries nothing in either direction. Creative F section 2 makes it 11.7 percent per segment under genome-wide correction and about 4.2 percent per whole chromosome on large autosomes; spread over five chromosomes a burden needs about 21 percent to be seen, over 22 it is undetectable. It is blood only, so a clone confined to kidney or muscle is invisible at any fraction. It neither establishes that a target exists, the lab geneticist's correct reason for gating, nor that it does not. Any aneuploidy-selective candidate must rest on per-tissue interphase FISH or single-cell sequencing of blood, buccal cells and skin. The negative binds one thing: a single large clonal trisomy in blood above about 4 percent.
