# Round 1, chromosomal instability biologist (panel 2)

My lens is selection. This child is a mosaic of euploid and aneuploid cells competing in every tissue. Aneuploid cells carry a measured fitness penalty: impaired proliferation across four mouse trisomies (Williams 2008, PMID 18974345), proteotoxic stress with impaired HSP90-dependent folding and a blunted heat-shock response (Oromendia 2012, PMID 23222101; Donnelly 2014, PMID 25205676), limited autophagic capacity with TFEB activation (Santaguida 2015, PMID 26404941), and p53-dependent arrest (Thompson and Compton 2010, PMID 20123995). Engineered trisomic lines grow poorly in vitro and as xenografts, so single-chromosome gains behave as tumour suppressors (Sheltzer 2017, PMID 28089890). Tumours arise when a clone acquires tolerance; HSF1 overexpression was the first aneuploidy-tolerating mutation found in human cells (Donnelly 2014).

Every candidate is scored first on direction. A drug relieving proteotoxic, energetic or lysosomal stress is a tolerance mutation delivered pharmacologically to the whole child, improving tissue function and raising tumour risk together. Only one direction is defensible as therapy: selection against the aneuploid compartment.

## 1. Ranked list

**1. Metformin, the approved stand-in for AICAR, tested ex vivo first.** Step: AMPK activation via complex I, the energy-stress arm of the aneuploidy-selective screen. Tier: cell. Tang 2011 (PMID 21315436) found AICAR killed trisomy 1, 13, 16 and 19 MEFs at 0.2 to 0.5 mM through p53-Ser15-Bax, and tested metformin, which "impaired the accumulation of trisomy 13 and 16 cells in culture, although the effects were not as dramatic"; the authors call the difference between the two AMPK activators unclear (verified in PMC3532042, correcting dossier 01 section 4.2). The one patient-cell precedent is AICAR removing isochromosome 12p cells from Pallister-Killian fibroblasts while euploid cells persisted (creative G5); the metformin arm was never run. Paediatric: approved from age 10, licensed oral solution UK and US, oncology phase I 666 to 2000 mg/m2/day (creative I). Phenotype: cystatin C not creatinine at low muscle mass, stop below eGFR 30. Harm: lactic acidosis on a renal background, appetite loss in a child not growing. Readout: FISH aneuploid fraction in blood, 22% detectable change at p0 = 0.10 (creative F3). Rank: the only approved drug with a published direction of effect against aneuploid cells in patient material.

**2. Rule: no chaperone inducer, chemical chaperone or rapalog for the syndrome.** Step: aneuploidy tolerance. Tier: cell, plus yeast for rapamycin. Phenylbutyrate, arimoclomol and teprenone raise folding capacity, the specific deficit of the aneuploid state, and aneuploid yeast grow better under rapamycin (dossier 02 section 5). Harm: tissue performance bought by better survival of the compartment that becomes the tumour. Readout: aneuploid versus euploid proliferation by FISH in patient fibroblasts. Rank: costless, and it removes candidates other panellists will bring.

**3. Rule: written aneugen avoidance, with a taxane and vinca flag.** Step: mis-segregation rate. Tier: human genotoxicity plus BubR1-knockdown cells. Colchicine is the OECD TG 487 reference aneugen (PMID 20362070), griseofulvin a spindle aneugen still used for tinea capitis (PMID 25308540), albendazole and mebendazole bind the same site (creative A10). Partial BubR1 reduction sensitises cells to low-dose taxol while Mad2 reduction does not (Janssen 2009, PMID 19855003), and mis-segregated chromosomes are damaged in the furrow, so the aneugen is also a mutagen (Janssen 2011, PMID 21960636). Harm: new aneuploid clones plus structural damage at 39 to 75% background cancer risk. Readout: micronucleus frequency before and four weeks after unavoidable exposure.

**4. Rule: exclude CDK4/6 inhibitors and senescence-blocking agents; treat senolysis as unproven.** Step: the p16 arrest. Tier: mouse. p16 ablation attenuates sarcopenia, fat loss and cataract in BubR1 hypomorphs while p19Arf ablation worsens them (Baker 2008, PMID 18516091), and INK-ATTAC clearance improved every measured outcome without substantially extending lifespan (Baker 2011, PMID 22048312). The selection reading differs from the ageing reading: mis-segregation produces arrested cells with complex karyotypes and pro-inflammatory signalling that drives immune clearance (Santaguida 2017, PMID 28633018). Palbociclib, ribociclib and abemaciclib, approved p16-axis hits in creative E, license aneuploid cells to keep dividing.

**5. Rule: no growth hormone, no mecasermin.** Step: growth signalling across the mosaic. Tier: human, other diseases. GH gave no response in the two MVA2 patients tested (PMID 36588761), mecasermin is contraindicated with a malignancy history, and GH in children at raised cancer risk is unresolved (Raman 2015, PMID 25839904). Carrier sarcopenia correlates with mTORC1 hyperactivity (Sieben 2020, PMID 31738183), so this axis is not obviously deficient. Harm: proliferation raised in both compartments. Rank: the society leaflet lists growth hormones as current treatment for short stature (dossier 03, M6).

**6. Hydroxychloroquine, last, not recommended for use.** Step: the lysosomal and autophagic dependency of aneuploid cells (Santaguida 2015; chloroquine selective but "not as dramatic", Tang 2011). Tier: cell. Paediatric: 5 mg/kg/day, 90-day stable suspension (creative I). Phenotype: retinal screening, QT stacking with ondansetron, renal dosing. Harm: the direction may invert in brain, where autophagy activation dampened CIN-induced neural stem cell damage in Drosophila (creative G6, PMID 41820377). Readout: the same FISH endpoint, ex vivo first.

## 2. Creative-round ideas

Adopted: AICAR-to-metformin substitution and the Pallister-Killian precedent (G5), the most useful new fact of the round. FISH over micronucleus counting as primary (F3). The aneugen list with named alternatives (B5, A10). Patient fibroblasts and parental lymphocytes as pre-gate (C1, C2, J3). Buccal micronucleus as family-collected secondary (J1). Cystatin C for all renal dosing (I). Residual rather than lifetime risk as the threshold (F4). Adopted with the sign reversed: the ceramide entry (B9) is right that direction decides, and improving aneuploid fitness is the direction to avoid.

Rejected: cardiac glycosides for NMD stabilisation (A1), since a stabilised transcript still stops at 737 and the NMD premise survived computation (D6). Amlexanox, ataluren and caffeine citrate (A2 to A4), on evidence rather than selection grounds. Arimoclomol, teprenone, phenylbutyrate (A5, A6, B8), by rule 2. Niacin, acipimox and nicotinamide for the SIRT2 axis (A7, C5, G2), since raising BubR1 in every cell also raises aneuploid fitness and the SIRT2 lifespan result is sex-specific and contradicted (Wu 2023, PMID 38009412). Ultra-low-dose paclitaxel (A9, JC), by rule 3. Pulsed dasatinib (B6), since no senolytic has been given to a BubR1 mouse and the growth plate is the compartment the child cannot spare (JA). Lamivudine and the interferon gate as treatment (B7, B1), since cGAS-STING output from micronuclei is part of the clearance constraining aneuploid clones (Bakhoum 2018, PMID 29342134).

## 3. Drugs excluded

Chaperone inducers, chemical chaperones and rapalogs for the syndrome (tolerance). CDK4/6 inhibitors and senomorphics (arrest bypass). GH and mecasermin. Colchicine, griseofulvin, albendazole, mebendazole, elective taxane or vinca. HSP90 inhibitors, directionally correct against aneuploid cells but destabilising the only BubR1 this child makes (Suijkerbuijk 2010, PMID 20516114) and unapproved outside Japan. Proteasome inhibitors, whose aneuploid dependency (Quinton 2021, PMID 33505027) is a vulnerability only when the aneuploid compartment is the tumour, which here it is not. Hydroxyurea, which raises micronucleated reticulocytes (PMC3277805). Acetazolamide and topiramate, renal. Ataluren, unobtainable. Mps1, Aurora B, PLK1 and KIF18A inhibitors, none approved and all directionally harmful here.

## 4. Strongest objection to my own top candidate

Selection against aneuploid cells assumes a euploid population remains to repopulate the tissue, which is unproven here. The sequenced sample bounds a single clonal trisomy in blood at roughly 4% and a variegated burden hardly at all, and a clone confined to kidney or muscle is invisible at any fraction (F2). If a tissue is largely aneuploid, an aneuploidy-selective antiproliferative subtracts cells from an organ with no reserve, in a child presenting with failure to thrive and low muscle mass. Tang 2011 killed trisomic MEFs through p53, and that pathway in a growing child acts on tissue, not on a tumour. Metformin was also the weaker AMPK activator in that paper, and AMPK activation is in general a survival response to energy stress, so the possibility that it improves tolerance rather than removing aneuploid cells is untested. A negative FISH result would not separate no effect from both compartments suppressed equally.

## 5. What would change my ranking

Direct measurement of the aneuploid fraction by FISH or single-cell sequencing on blood, buccal cells and skin, which says whether a euploid reserve exists per tissue. A metformin arm of the Pallister-Killian fibroblast experiment, or the same design in MVA fibroblasts. Any measurement of aneuploid versus euploid proliferation under a chaperone inducer or rapalog in patient cells, which tests rule 2 rather than asserting it. A published residual cancer risk for a treated MVA1 child (F4). Evidence that immune clearance of missegregating cells is rate-limiting in MVA, from interferon score and NK phenotyping, which would open a class I have no candidate for.
