# Round 2: mitosis / cell biology

## (a) Objections

**Geneticist.** Top candidate, potassium citrate. He concedes it ("not a BUB1B repurposing hypothesis", round1_geneticist §Strongest objection). My addition: his rank 2, "deferred pharmacotherapy pending three assays", is not a market-approved medication and consumes one of six slots the brief reserves for them; it is a gate on the list, not an entry in it. Second claim: that a wild-type-like assay result for p.Asn1002Lys "makes this a monoallelic case" (§What would change my ranking). Monoallelic is not a stable category here: the García-Castillo monoallelic group was resolved as carrying a second allele 44 kb upstream of a transcription start site, confirmed by TALEN knock-in (Ochiai 2014, PMID 24344301; 01_mechanism_models.md §2.3). A normal in-vitro result on N1002K would move the search to regulatory sequence, not close the genotype.

**Oncologist.** Top candidate, hydrochlorothiazide plus citrate: same objection, which he also concedes. Second claim: dexrazoxane at rank 2, justified by BubR1 mice dying of cardiac failure (round1_oncologist §1). Dexrazoxane acts on topoisomerase IIβ and iron-mediated anthracycline injury. Nothing links that lesion to the BubR1 cardiac phenotype, where the one reversed readout, the ECG J-point defect, was corrected by a SIRT2 transgene (North 2014, PMID 24825348; 01 §4.1). The rank imports a mouse cause of death into an unrelated drug mechanism, and topoisomerase II exposure carries a secondary MDS/AML question in a syndrome where MDS with monosomy 7 is reported (02_clinical_care_drugs.md §1).

**Pharmacologist.** Top candidate, hydroxychloroquine, with micronuclei per 1,000 binucleated lymphocytes as the primary endpoint and a pre-specified 30% fall (§N-of-1 protocol). This is a mechanism-endpoint mismatch. Micronucleus frequency scores ongoing mis-segregation. Chloroquine's activity in Tang 2011 was selective killing of cells already aneuploid (PMID 21315436; 01 §5), with no proposed action on kinetochore attachment or checkpoint strength, so a fall in micronuclei is not a predicted effect and its absence would not falsify the hypothesis. Second claim: dasatinib "approved from age 1 ... with a powder for oral suspension at 72 mg/m² approved alongside the tablet in November 2017", cited to a news page. 02 §5 records only the weight-banded tablet on 9 Nov 2017. Two approvals are merged and the source is not a label.

**Family representative.** Top candidate, thiazide plus citrate, conceded as "not repurposing". Second claim: the rule to "refuse any label carrying growth, immunity or neoplasm language" (§Acceptable-risk position). As a filter it removes the only candidate whose target has a causal intervention in this gene's own mouse (Baker 2008 PMID 18516091; Baker 2011 PMID 22048312; 01 §4.1). The growth warning derives from continuous daily CML dosing; the senolytic design is intermittent, three days per month, on the hit-and-run principle of Zhu 2015, where one dose held for 7 months (01 §4.2). Label language and exposure are not the same object.

## (b) What changes my ranking

The pharmacologist's endpoint objection applies to my own file: I gave micronucleus frequency as a readout for metformin and for candidate 2. Error-rate endpoints (micronuclei, PCS fraction) test nothing about the aneuploidy-selective agents; only burden endpoints (interphase FISH, low-pass single-cell sequencing) do, and blood shows no clone above ~10% (PROMPT_track2.md, Facts). The oncologist's Sudo 2004 point (PMID 15059905) corrects my harm wording: SAC suppression yields taxane resistance as well as error amplification, so the direction is unpredictable rather than uniformly toward hypersensitivity. Four colleagues ranked renal management first; I accept that it is the only entry with a measured target, while recording that it tests no BubR1 hypothesis.

Revised list:
1. **Potassium citrate ± hydrochlorothiazide**, gated on urine chemistry. Up from 6, on the measured-target argument made by all four colleagues.
2. **Dasatinib, intermittent, three days per month.** Down from 1: unchanged mechanism, but Baker 2011 showed senolytic benefit stopping short of the cardiac cause of death, so the expected ceiling is lower; add echocardiography.
3. **Metformin.** Up from 4: on burden endpoints it and HCQ are equivalent in tier, and its toxicity does not land on an organ the syndrome already damages.
4. **Hydroxychloroquine.** Down from 2, on the mechanism-endpoint mismatch and on the eye.
5. **Sirolimus or everolimus.** Down from 3: three colleagues exclude it, and the proteostasis direction and immunosuppression arguments both point away.
6. **Removed: pimitespib.** Japan-only, no paediatric formulation, and inhibiting HSP90 would further destabilise residual BubR1 (01 §2.2).

## (c) Factual error

round1_family.md §3 states that hydroxychloroquine "retinopathy risk is low under age five", reading 02 §5 as a statement about the child's age. Those figures are exposure durations, not ages: risk negligible below 6 months and unlikely below 5 years of treatment at 5 mg/kg/day or less (02 §5, from J AAPOS 2023, S1091-8531(22)00330-5). The proposal is chronic dosing from early childhood, so the number that governs is accumulated years on the drug.

## (d) The disagreement not to paper over

Whether senescence and p53-dependent arrest here are pathology to remove or the main brake on karyotypically complex cells. I rank dasatinib high on mechanism; my colleagues rank it 4th to 6th on paediatric harm. Averaging those into "dasatinib, mid-list" hides that nobody has addressed the tumour direction: mis-segregating cells arrest, acquire senescence features and are cleared through inflammatory signalling (Santaguida 2017, PMID 28633018), against a reported cancer risk of 38.7% to about 75% (02 §1). One experiment settles it, a senolytic in BubR1^H/H or ^H/L1002P mice with tumour incidence as a co-primary endpoint, and it has not been done.

Related note, not a disagreement: four of five files name micronucleus frequency as the primary readout. For the aneuploidy-selective candidates that is a shared error, not a consensus.
