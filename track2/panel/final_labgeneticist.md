## 1. Errors

**The RT-PCR failure mode is stated backwards.** Section 6(a)2: "under efficient decay the c.2210 allele is depleted from the cDNA, so its absence mimics cis". Decay does the opposite. In trans the surviving transcript carries c.3006G, so cDNA shows c.3006G near 100 per cent. In cis both changes sit on one molecule, decay removes both, and c.3006G falls near zero. Opposite signatures. The real limit is narrower: under efficient decay phase cannot be read off a single molecule and must be inferred from the c.3006 fraction. The block arm is still not optional; the stated reason for it is wrong.

**The rest of gate item 2 is stated correctly**: primer placement, the 950 to 1,150 bp product across six junctions, 500 consensus reads, both parents plus three controls plus a synthetic 1:1 mix, and the NMD-block condition as a paired stimulated lymphocyte or lymphoblastoid culture in vehicle against cycloheximide 100 micrograms per mL for 4 to 6 h. One correction to the sample requirement: "It needs living cells" holds only for the block arm; phase and allele-specific expression run from stabilised RNA in a PAXgene tube drawn at a scheduled visit.
