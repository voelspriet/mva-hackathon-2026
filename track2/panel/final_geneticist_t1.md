## 2. Overreach

**Round4 §6(a) item 2: the RT-PCR "Decides phase, allele-specific expression and NMD efficiency together".** Its own author says otherwise: under efficient decay the c.2210 allele is depleted from cDNA, and round2_labgeneticist §e2 adds that amplification bias over a 900 bp product makes the ratio non-quantitative without the synthetic 1:1 control, and that the cycloheximide comparison is qualitative. Required wording: "Returns NMD efficiency and, where the mutant allele is detectable in cDNA, phase. Where decay is efficient the phase result is not interpretable, and phase must come from parental genotypes or long-read genomic sequencing."

**Round4 §4, the 731X row: "Kills the rejection of transcript stabilisation".** V1 §2(d) attaches three boundaries the row drops: 731X encodes 715 residues, no construct terminating at 737 was ever tested, and every result is transient replacement in HeLa and U2OS. The 2012 authors write that "generation of a vertebrate organism expressing the equivalent of BUBR1-731X will be needed". Required wording: add "in cultured human cells; never tested in an organism, and never at codon 737".

## 3. Omission

Round4 §6(c) entry 5 carries "cyclophosphamide with mesna for ifosfamide" without the caveat I filed in round2_geneticist §a: RMS 2005 randomised patients in remission after nine IVA-based cycles (02 §4), so the 85.0 against 72.4 percent maintenance figure sits downstream of ifosfamide induction and does not transfer unchanged to a VAC induction. It matters because the entry is the only place in the report where a chemotherapy backbone is altered on this panel's advice.

## 4. The verified facts

**Metformin.** V1 §1b changes my position. I ranked it third in round 2. Tang tested 0.5 and 1.0 mM in Ts13 and Ts16 MEFs for 72 h, called the effect "subtle", and reported no IC50, no human line and no xenograft. Against paediatric plasma of 7.7 to 15.5 micromolar the gap is 32 to 130 fold. I accept the demotion to round4's rank 6, an ex vivo pre-gate, and withdraw metformin as a prescription candidate.

**The 731X result.** V1 §2(a) and §2(d) move it from the 2012 paper to Cancer Res 2010, with rescue of checkpoint, alignment and segregation and expression at 1.44 times wild type once NMD is bypassed. This does not revive digoxin, refused on therapeutic index. It changes the ordering inside the gate: if the transcript is the lesion and its product would work, NMD efficiency on the c.2210 allele is a more informative first number than the BubR1 immunoblot, which cannot say why the protein is low. I would run item 2 before item 4.

**Coordinator question 1.** The single amplicon complements parental phasing; it does not replace it. Parental genotyping at two sites 10.9 kb apart is a segregation test immune to the artefact above, and it also detects a de novo allele. Order both from one visit: parental Sanger for phase, the RT-PCR with the paired cycloheximide arm for NMD and allele-specific expression. If only one can be run, run the RT-PCR, because it is the only one returning a number nobody has for any MVA patient, and accept that a negative c.2210 result leaves phase open.

**Coordinator question 2.** I accept the gate and reject the word overturned. The two competing causes flip the thiazide, not the alkali: hypocitraturia follows both prematurity-associated nephrocalcinosis and ifosfamide proximal acidosis, and potassium citrate is defensible under either while the tubular panel returns. What the nephrologist has established is that the thiazide was ungated in three team 1 files, including mine, and that the ultrasound trajectory over two years, not a six-week urine ratio, is the endpoint. I also note the ifosfamide arm cannot be assessed at all: the chemotherapy regimen and cumulative dose are not in any file we were given.

## 5. Sign-off

Section 6 represents my position, with one sentence I want printed as dissent:

> One test does not settle phase: where nonsense-mediated decay is efficient, the mutant allele disappears from the cDNA and its absence is indistinguishable from cis, so parental genotypes or long-read genomic sequencing must run alongside the RT-PCR, not instead of it.
