## 2. Overreach

**Section 2(vii), rapamycin.** "Aneuploid growth under rapamycin" traces to dossier 02 section 5, where the entry reads "yeast data" with no PMID, and it went through neither verification pass. Required wording: "reported in budding yeast disomes, not verified against a primary source here and not shown in human cells."

**Section 4, Baker 2013 row.** "Revives the expression-directed arm" is stronger than the finding. Required wording: "removes the mechanistic objection to raising BubR1; it supplies no evidence that any approved NAD+ precursor raises BubR1 in this genotype, since the NMN experiment was seven days, intraperitoneal, in wild-type mice, with a clean readout only in testes (V2 section 3b)."

**Section 6(c) item 6, the metformin ladder.** 5, 15, 50 and 500 micromolar with an AICAR control cannot interpret a null, since the only concentrations at which metformin has a published effect are 500 and 1,000 micromolar (V1 section 1b). Add 1,000 micromolar as a metformin-internal positive control, or the experiment cannot separate "does not work at reachable exposure" from "does not work in this cell type".

## 3. Omission

The variegated-burden correction is used in the gate but never stated as a reading rule, and section 10 does not list it among the blind spots. It matters because "mosaic aneuploidy not detected above about 10 percent" will be read as "he is mostly euploid". Per creative F section 2 it bounds a single clonal trisomy in blood near 4.2 percent, needs about 21 percent total burden to be visible if spread over five chromosomes, is blind above 22, and is blind at any fraction to a clone confined to kidney or muscle.

Second: section 6(d) bars chaperone inducers "pending disagreement 5" without the reason, that single-chromosome gains behave as tumour suppressors and tumours arise when a clone acquires tolerance (Sheltzer 2017, PMID 28089890; Donnelly 2014, PMID 25205676). Without it the bar reads as arbitrary.

## 4. The verified facts

Four changes, all against me.

**Metformin.** I withdraw "Tang 2011 gives no concentration for metformin". It was tested at 0.5 and 1.0 mM in Ts13 and Ts16 MEFs at 72 hours (V1 section 1b), so the gap against paediatric plasma of 7.7 to 15.5 micromolar is 32 to 130 fold, wider than the PK seat argued. I accept rank 6, ex vivo only.

**Bakhoum 2018.** Withdrawn as my citation. Clearance of untransformed aneuploid cells stands on Santaguida 2017 (PMID 28633018) and Wang 2021 (PMID 34105235), which is the immunologist's ground, not mine.

**731X.** I withdraw the mechanism objection to transcript stabilisation. A truncation removing the kinase domain rescues checkpoint, alignment and unperturbed segregation and is expressed at 1.44 times wild type once NMD is bypassed (V1 section 2a, PMID 20516114). The remaining objection to digoxin is therapeutic index, as round4 records.

**Baker 2013.** It does not change my rule; it splits it, and the half it kills was not the load-bearing half. My bar was on agents that raise the fitness of cells already aneuploid without reducing the rate at which new ones are made. Raising BubR1 does the opposite: it corrects checkpoint and attachment defects, lowering production at source, and Baker 2013 accordingly reports reduced aneuploidy and reduced tumorigenesis against oncogenic Ras. Tolerance and correction are opposite interventions, and my round 1 wording did not separate them. The bar on agents that raise BubR1 is therefore withdrawn, and with it my rejection of the NAD+ and SIRT2 arm on that ground. The bar on chaperone inducers, chemical chaperones and rapalogs stands, because HSF1 capacity is the documented aneuploidy-tolerating change in human cells (Donnelly 2014) and none of those agents touches the mis-segregation rate. Two cautions carry with the withdrawal: Baker 2013 is a germline transgene at supra-normal level on a wild-type background, which no approved drug reproduces, and the link from an NAD+ precursor to BubR1 in this genotype is untested.
