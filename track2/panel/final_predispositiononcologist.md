**731X: my rejection reasoning was wrong.** I rejected transcript stabilisation because a stabilised transcript "still terminates at 737 and yields no kinase domain". The mechanism is live. My conclusion holds on the ground section 6(d) states correctly: digoxin is refused on therapeutic index. Ranking unchanged, premise replaced.

## 5. Sign-off

Section 6 represents my position, with one exception. The 6(b) carve-out reads "curative-intent, relapse or protocol-defined", which covers maintenance through the named vinorelbine, and covers relapse. It does not name disease-control or palliative intent, and its protection runs through a finite list of drug names, so vinblastine, topotecan, doxorubicin and etoposide are unprotected by omission. Dissent, for printing:

> The carve-out protects only what it names, so it must be repeated verbatim wherever an antineoplastic agent appears in this report, section 6(d) included, and it must read "any antineoplastic agent given with curative, maintenance, relapse, disease-control or palliative intent", because a closed list of protected drug names is what allows a regimen to be de-escalated by a clinician who will never see another case of this disease.
