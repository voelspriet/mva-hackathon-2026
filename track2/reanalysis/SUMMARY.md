# Re-analysis of the primary data: what was asked, what was found, what is left

Run under `track2/audit/PROMPT_B_reanalysis.md`, after the audit round and both cross-model passes. Every angle below was computed from data already in hand. No patient data left this machine; gnomAD was read as a static public file by byte range, never queried with a patient coordinate.

R7 is the only one that returned anything at all, and what it returned changes a reason rather than a conclusion.

## What this does not do

It does not change any recommendation in `REPORT_track2.md`. Fourteen negatives and one methodological hole are the whole of it. R8 is the first to correct a deliverable other than the report, and what it corrected was a sentence promising that an answer could be looked up for free. R7 corrects the stated *reason* for one assumption without changing the assumption, and it declines to turn its one novel finding into advice. The one place the report should be touched is the mosaicism detection floor, which is now known to be a genome-average figure that fails in low-mappability regions, and that qualification belongs beside the figure.
