## Production notes

All twelve visuals are built from material already in this repository or from plain text cards. No stock imagery, no photographs, no images of any person, nothing that identifies anyone. Every screenshot is of a file in the repo, captured at a size where the text is legible at 1080p.

**V1. Opening title card.** Black background, white sans-serif. Three lines, fading in one at a time: "One child." / "Two changes in one gene." / "*BUB1B*." No variant nomenclature on screen in this beat. No logo, no name, no date.

**V2. The three constraints.** Title card, same style. Line 1: "No approved medicine acts on BubR1." Line 2: "No repurposed drug has ever been given to a person with this syndrome, for the syndrome." Line 3, held on screen and in a heavier weight: "Nothing here is expected to reduce this child's cancer risk." Small source line at the foot: REPORT_track2.md section 1.

**V3. The two panels.** Screen capture of a file listing of `track2/panel/` and `track2/panel2/` side by side, showing the round files and the seat names. Nothing is opened. The point is the count and the separation, so mark the two columns "Team 1, five seats" and "Team 2, eight seats" and add the caption "blind to each other".

**V4. Divergent round, verification, referees.** One slow horizontal pan across three file listings: `track2/creative/` showing A through J, `track2/verify/` showing the verification passes, and `track2/referees/` showing the three referee reports. Caption at the foot, appearing on the third listing: "three referees, all returned major revision".

**V5. Interstitial card.** One line, centred: "Verification and refereeing removed the two strongest candidates."

**V6. Metformin.** Screenshot of the metformin paragraph in section 7 of the report. Highlight two fragments only: "7.7 to 15.5 micromolar" with "a gap of 32 to 130 fold", and "the ceiling is toxicological". Add a small caption: "the residual gap at the toxic ceiling is still 12.9 to 25.8 fold".

**V7. Dasatinib.** Screenshot of the dasatinib paragraph in section 7, with the three objections numbered 1, 2, 3 in the margin as an overlay: tumour direction unresolved, growth deceleration through cartilage, immune clearance suppressed. Caption: "each objection fails the candidate on its own".

**V8. The two corrections.** Two stacked cards, both in the report's own wording.
Card A, from section 13: "85.0 against 72.4 percent" struck through, "86.5 against 73.7 percent" beside it, and beneath it "primary endpoint, disease-free survival, p=0.061, not significant". Footer: "found by the referees. Inside the safety carve-out."
Card B, from section 2: "731X = nonsense allele" struck through, "IVS16-2A>G, splice acceptor, p.Ser714fsTer731" beside it. Footer: "found by neither panel nor any referee. The readthrough argument built on it is withdrawn."

**V9. The carve-out.** Full-width screenshot of the block quote in section 5, scrolled so the DOES NOT APPLY TO sentence is centred and complete. No highlight, no annotation. It should be readable as printed.

**V10. Four surveillance additions.** A card built from section 6 item 2, four numbered lines in the report's own order, with a right-hand column reading "no cost" against each: UGT1A1 before any irinotecan, bladder and vagina added to the existing scan, no assumed end date at age 7, full blood count on a draw already scheduled. Small footnote in lighter type, carried from the report: "the UGT1A1 star 28 repeat is called unreliably by short-read sequencing".

**V11. The gate.** Screenshot of section 4, positioned to show item 1, the tubular panel, and item 4, the BubR1 immunoblot. Overlay a single pull quote on item 4, in the report's words: "a figure that exists for no MVA patient".

**V12. The tool, then the close.** Two parts in one beat. First, the pipeline output: a terminal line running `pipeline/track2_drugmap/drugmap.py` against `targets_bub1b.json`, then the first rows of `track2/creative/E_drugmap_output.csv` rendered as a table with the rank, drug and harm-flag columns visible, and a caption giving the run figures from section 3: "110 targets, 148 molecules, 29 approved, 350 seconds". Include the report's own line about the top hit in small type: "its top hit is its own limitation. It is a shortlist for a human." Second, cut to a closing card holding the last sentence of the narration, then a final static frame: "Track 2. A hypothesis for follow-up. Not evidence that a medicine works."

**Optional cutaway, if a beat needs air.** `figures/mosaic_profile.png` from Track 1, shown without annotation, under V3 or V5. It carries no identifiers. Do not label it with anything beyond "Track 1 output".

**Things deliberately not shown.** No karyotype, no scan, no sequence file, no patient photograph or silhouette, no age, sex, country or centre. No music that reads as either uplifting or ominous. No drug name appears on screen as a recommendation without the carve-out or the exclusion that governs it.
