Method as specified in `PROMPT_A_source_audit.md`. Claims already adjudicated in V1, V2 or V3 were re-checked against source rather than carried across. No claim below is marked SUPPORTED on the basis of another file in this project, a review, or a secondary citation. Numbers were recomputed. Where the primary source could not be reached the verdict is UNVERIFIABLE and the routes attempted are listed.

Access levels used: **label** (openFDA current SPL, retrieved live), **SmPC** (emc, retrieved live), **regulator record** (EMA medicine page, EMA referral page, FDA GRAS notice, ClinicalTrials.gov API v2), **PMC full text**, **publisher abstract** (PubMed/NLM), **PMID record** (esummary title/journal/year only), **not reached**.
