# Prompt E: external read of the challenge data and what was derived from it

## What is attached

Material from one entry to an open scientific challenge. The challenge supplied a whole-genome variant file and a clinical phenotype document for one child. The attached files are the phenotype terms as supplied, and the measurements and summaries the entrant derived from the supplied data. Variant coordinates, sample identifiers and protein changes have been replaced with stable tokens; the reasoning is unaffected by the substitution.

## Task

Three parts. Answer each independently. Where a part cannot be answered from the attached material, say which material would be needed.

### Part 1: statements that cannot both be true

List pairs of statements in the attached material that are incompatible. For each pair give both statements quoted, the file each appears in, and what would have to be the case for both to hold. Include pairs where one statement is a measurement and the other is an assumption made elsewhere about the same quantity. Do not rank the pairs and do not say which matters more.

### Part 2: questions the attached data supports asking

List questions that the attached measurements make answerable, or make worth answering, and that the attached material does not already answer. For each:

- the question
- which attached measurement makes it askable
- what data would answer it, and whether that data appears to be in hand, obtainable, or absent
- what each possible answer would change

Exclude questions that require contacting the family, the child's clinicians, or the challenge organisers. Exclude questions already listed as open in the attached material, unless the attached measurements change what they would cost or what they would settle.

### Part 3: what is absent, and what is present but not visible

Two lists.

**3a.** Statements in the attached material that a reader with domain expertise would find unsupported, overstated, or missing a step. Quote each and say what is missing.

**3b.** Content in the attached material that a reader of the main report alone would not encounter, with a note on whether its absence from the report changes how the report should be read.

Do not assess quality, do not compare this entry to any other, and do not offer encouragement or advice about presentation.

## Constraints on the answer

Quote rather than paraphrase when attributing a statement to a file. Where you infer, label the inference. Where a claim in the attached material appears correct, say nothing about it; report only what fails one of the three tests above. State any part of the task you did not complete and why. No em or en dashes.
