# AUDIT (c): clinical oncology, surveillance and rhabdomyosarcoma

Partition (c) of the source audit specified in `track2/audit/PROMPT_A_source_audit.md`.

Scope audited: MVA natural history and case counts; cancer spectrum and frequencies; the 38.7 percent and 75 percent cancer-risk figures; surveillance guidelines, their intervals and horizons; radiation avoidance; rhabdomyosarcoma regimens; the RMS 2005 maintenance trial figures and endpoints; chemotherapy toxicity reports in MVA; MDS and leukaemia in MVA; the genitourinary tumour site claim; the adult-onset phenotype.

Out of scope and not audited here: BubR1 molecular and mouse biology; aneuploidy, senescence and immune surveillance; nephrology, endocrinology and nutrition; pharmacology and regulatory status; variant interpretation and databases; the power and variance arithmetic in section 8 of the report (the cancer-risk inputs to that arithmetic are audited, the arithmetic is not).

Files read in full or in part: `REPORT_track2.md`, `track2/research/01_mechanism_models.md`, `track2/research/02_clinical_care_drugs.md`, `track2/creative/B_consequence_tissue_schedule.md`, `track2/creative/F_math.md`, `track2/creative/G_osint.md`, `track2/creative/J_lateral.md`, `track2/panel/round4_grand_moderator.md`, `track2/panel/final_predispositiononcologist.md`, `track2/panel/final_geneticist_t1.md`, `track2/verify/V3_referee_challenges.md`, `track2/gate/G3_dose_response.md`.

## 6. Observations that are not verdicts

Recorded because they bear on how the audited claims should be read, without being errors in any claim.

1. **The RMS 2005 correction propagated to the report and not to the working deliverables.** `V3_referee_challenges.md` adjudicated the figures correctly against Bisogno 2019, and REPORT_track2.md sections 5, 6 and 7 carry the corrected numbers. Four files still in the deliverable list carry the superseded 85.0 against 72.4 percent: `02_clinical_care_drugs.md:78`, `B_consequence_tissue_schedule.md:47`, `round4_grand_moderator.md:51` and `:78`, and `final_geneticist_t1.md:17`. The moderator file carries it inside its own version of the carve-out.
2. **Where 85.0 and 72.4 come from is not established.** Neither figure appears in Bisogno 2019 nor in the RMS 2005 long-term analysis (PMID 40053891, 10-year OS 82.9 versus 70.8 percent). The same applies to the paired DFS figures 78.1 and 70.1 percent at `02_clinical_care_drugs.md:78`.
3. **Pavone 2022 already advises the surveillance the report proposes as an addition.** REPORT_track2.md section 6(d) proposes a full blood count and states that no haematological surveillance appeared in the earlier draft. Pavone 2022, cited two lines earlier for the malignancy spectrum, states: "Abdominal ultrasound, fundus examination, and hematological checkup are advised every 3 months." The proposal is not thereby wrong, but a cited source already recommends it, and the report does not say so.
4. **Orphanet's 75 percent is a May 2012 record.** The report prints it beside a 2022 figure without dating it, and the two therefore look more comparable than they are. The report does state that the two are unreconciled and probably ascertainment-driven.
5. **Pavone's own denominator is internally inconsistent.** The running text says "In the group of 30 MVA patients" while the Table 1 caption says 31 and every percentage in the sentence resolves on 31. The project's use of 31 is the arithmetically consistent choice.
6. **Pavone's index patient carries a single heterozygous BUB1B variant** (c.2679A>T, p.Arg893Ser), so the label MVA1 on the 31-case series is Pavone's framing. Variant interpretation is partition (f); the point is recorded here only because the 38.7 percent denominator rests on that series.
7. **Garcia-Castillo's severe subgroup is the monoallelic one.** Its Wilms figure of 7/8 is the highest tumour frequency quoted anywhere in this project's files, and it belongs to a group the same paper distinguishes from the biallelic patients, who "showed a moderate phenotype".
8. **Two access notes for checkability.** Schneider 2020's full text is at PMC7658107, which no project file records. Orphanet's record is behind a bot wall on the HTML page and was reached through a text-extraction proxy of the same URL.
